Our Story

Amentrop was founded with a vision to solve one of the biggest challenges in modern hiring — the disconnect between academic learning and industry expectations.

While companies struggle to find job-ready candidates, millions of students struggle to prove their real capabilities. We decided to bridge that gap through skill-based training, performance verification, and intelligent recruitment systems.

Today, Amentrop is evolving into a premium ecosystem where students, HRs, colleges, and businesses collaborate through technology-driven innovation.
